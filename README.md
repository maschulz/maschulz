I work on the reliability and limits of machine learning in medicine: what a model can and cannot extract from biomedical data, and what has to hold before its output supports a clinical conclusion. I lead the [Translational Barriers group](https://hertie.ai/data-science/research-groups/about/translational-barriers) at the [Hertie Institute for AI in Brain Health](https://hertie.ai), University of Tübingen.
